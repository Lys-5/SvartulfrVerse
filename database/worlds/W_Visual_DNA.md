## Global Aesthetic Profile (Argon / Gemini Standard)

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Style | Detailed anime / Korean manhwa illustration, semi-realistic digital painting, bold clean linework, rich cel-shaded and painterly rendering, dramatic lighting |
| Framing | Cowboy shot (mid-thigh or waist-up portrait), confident posture, cinematic depth of field |
| Lighting | Warm golden-orange sunset glow (`warm golden-hour rim lighting`), twilight violet/amber gradients, or moody nocturnal neon/moonlight contrast |
| Palette | Warm sunset amber, deep obsidian black, crimson/burgundy accents, slate grey, metallic silver/gold details |
| Anatomy Rule | **Single Pair of Ears Invariant**: When in Partial Shift (`wolf ears and tail visible`), characters have **ONLY ONE PAIR OF EARS** (the wolf ears on top of the head) and **NO human ears on the sides of the head** (`only one pair of ears, no human ears, hair covering the sides of the head where human ears would be`). In Human Form (e.g., Public/School/Suit), they have normal human ears and no wolf ears/tail. |

## Validation Rules

Reject immediately:
- **Double Ears Bug:** Any Partial Shift illustration showing human ears on the sides of the head in addition to wolf ears on top.
- **Generic Chibi / Flat Anime:** Lacking the semi-realistic manhwa proportions, muscular definition, and dramatic lighting.
- **Identity Drift:** Wrong eye color (e.g., amber instead of emerald-green for Jasper/Alyssa, or dark hair instead of snow-white for Alyssa/Wulfnic).
