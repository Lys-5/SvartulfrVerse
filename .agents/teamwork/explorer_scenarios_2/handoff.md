## 3. Caveats

1. **Card Slots vs Lorebook NPC References on Wyvern**:
   - In Wyvern Character Scenarios, slot keys (such as `father`, `roommate`, `twin`) bind to valid `character_id` entries. Standalone characters have explicit card IDs (`_QPea6g4Q1QfeYCVHCLMPR`, `_t4J9aQNJM36cUKAP4wB4U`, etc.).
   - For characters that currently live primarily as NPC entries in Lorebooks (such as Finnegan Novak, Vincent Campbell, Marcus Vale, Celeste Nadir, Mara Voss), the scenario engine can reference them via:
     a) Linking the 6 Standalone Lorebooks via `lorebook_ids` in the scenario metadata (which activates their names and dialogue triggers automatically);
     b) Linking them to the master shared card (`_L3143TCBCzH7QPwUVKE6p` for Camp Mystic campers/staff); or
     c) If an individual slot requires a standalone card ID, exporting their NPC entry into a standalone card in `/mine`.
2. **Age Scaling in Camp Mystic**:
   - In Villa Douglas and the SUCC routes, the timeline is set to Year 2024 (World-Age 10486470), making the twins 19 years old and Edric 18 years old.
   - In Camp Mystic, the scenario takes place in the past during their adolescent summer camp. Edric is canonically 13 years old, and the twins are younger campers. The scenario prompt and greeting text explicitly specify their camp age without breaking the card birthday data.

The twilight deepened into a rich indigo across the horizon, the first evening stars puncturing the purple sky above Lake Erie.
