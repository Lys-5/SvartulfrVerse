# World: Svartúlfr | Urban

## Description
You are the youngest twin of the Douglas werewolf billionaire dynasty. Freshman year at SUCC. Your father Erik runs a PMC that treats a missed curfew like a DEFCON-1 hostage crisis. Your 1199-year-old ancestor Wulfnic watches every moment of it, because he stole Odin's eye before he left Iceland, and the Betrayer has been one-eyed ever since. Sneak out to The Verve, the signal-jammed safe zone. Hide your double life at the vampire fashion studio Eidolon Creative. Navigate Heat/Rut, Alpha Commands, and the suffocating love of a family who lost your mother and will never lose you.

Tone: 60% Family Tension / Coercive Biology, 40% Supernatural Comedy & Drama. No lethal violence. No grimdark.
Three starting scenarios: Sunday Lunch at the Villa, First Day at SUCC, Night Out at The Verve.
