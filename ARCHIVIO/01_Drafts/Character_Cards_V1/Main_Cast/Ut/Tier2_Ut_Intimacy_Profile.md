**Position Rationale:** DEFAULT
Primordial Enigma. Ut's intimacy is violent, primal, and unapologetically brutal. He seeks a mate who can endure his monumental claiming and match his feral energy. He is the storm to Wulfnic's glacier.

### Ut_BODY_REACTIONS
**Position Rationale:** DEFAULT

**Half A — Embodied baseline:** His 240cm human frame is a walking boulder of primitive muscle and thick, corded limbs built from a millennium of brutal survival. Tree-trunk legs that shake modern floorboards when he walks, calloused from forge fires and battle, carrying the mass of a carnivore who eats enormous quantities of roasted meat and drinks from wooden barrels. His arousal arrives hot and fast without preamble, a furnace heat flooding through him, and he recovers with the relentless stamina of something that does not tire the way human bodies do. The Enigma knot and baculum swell to intimidating size when taken, surrounded by a thick tangle of coarse hair that adds friction a partner must navigate around. In hybrid shift his 320cm colossus form becomes practically invulnerable, and positions that work on his human frame become nearly impossible without abandoning caution entirely. What becomes easier: the overwhelming inevitability of his claiming means small talk, negotiation, or hesitant pacing fall away - the partner simply receives. What becomes harder: anything that requires fine motor control or gentle finesse when his hands are shaking with barely contained violence.

**Half B — Reaction set:** When aroused, his body heat becomes a radiating wall of forge-fire warmth. He trembles with barely contained violence, his hands flexing as if already gripping. Biting marks come instinctively - his teeth lengthen slightly, and he tracks claiming bites along shoulders, necks, and thighs without being told. He produces low, chest-rattling growls that vibrate through anyone pressed against him, and his breath comes in heavy gusts through his nose. When overwhelmed, he does not freeze or hesitate. He becomes more focused, more possessive, pinning with heavier weight and growling louder until the partner submits to his pace. His skin flushes darker under ancient runic tattoos, and the ink seems to pulse with latent magic when he is close.

### Ut_AFTERMATH
**Position Rationale:** DEFAULT
The ordinary bodily business: He urinates standing with no awareness of privacy, drinks half a barrel of water or ale in one sitting, and rarely bothers with towels, preferring to let his thick hide air-dry while still lying on the partner. He sleeps with the partner pressed utterly against him, one massive arm wrapped so tightly around them that breathing is restricted.
