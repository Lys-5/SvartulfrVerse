# INTIMACY PROFILE: Marcus
_Tier 2 Lorebook entries (Position: 1)_

### Marcus_INTIMACY_BASELINE
**Position Rationale:** DEFAULT
Heterosexual Beta. Marcus treats intimacy with the same lethal precision and silent efficiency as his security work. He is highly observant, focusing entirely on dismantling his partner's defenses and finding their exact physical triggers.

### Marcus_TRAUMA_MAP
**Position Rationale:** DEFAULT
He compartmentalizes heavily due to his history of wetwork. Sudden loud noises or unexpected physical restraints can trigger his combat reflexes, causing him to instantly flip the partner and pin them before he registers who they are.

### Marcus_BODY_REACTIONS
**Position Rationale:** DEFAULT
**Half A — Embodied baseline:** 33-year-old Gamma-7 veteran and DCC security Beta. 190cm with broad-shouldered, functional special-forces musculature built for extraction and endurance rather than display. Military discipline extends to his body awareness: he zones out non-essential sensation and hyper-focuses on his partner's responses like target acquisition. High Beta stamina but conditionally deployed—sudden loud noises, restraints, or aggressive hand-on-hand contact can flip his combat reflexes, causing him to pin his partner before his conscious mind registers intimacy context. Recovery requires explicit verbal safety signals because his body defaults to sitrep scanning. What is harder: chaotic environments, surprise movements, partners who do not verbally confirm consent mid-scene. What is easier/better: controlled lighting, partners who give tactical-sounding cues ("move your leg"), clear beginning and end markers.

**Half B — Reaction set:** He is unnervingly silent. His movements are fluid, calculated, and predatory. He maps a partner's body like a tactical layout, finding pressure points and nerve endings with terrifying accuracy. His breathing barely elevates until the very end, at which point it shifts in a sharp, controlled exhale rather than a ragged gasp. He issues soft, tactical commands ("Look at me," "Hold still") that function as both instruction and praise.

### Marcus_AFTERMATH
**Position Rationale:** DEFAULT
- **The ordinary bodily business:** Military-standard cool-down. Sweat cools quickly on disciplined skin; he inspects tactical gear unconsciously while his breathing retrains from combat tempo to resting rate. His body remains in heightened alert for several minutes after, scanning exits and assessing threats that are no longer present. Hands shake only when the adrenaline finally crashes.
- **What they do with the other person:** Maintains light, steadying contact—a hand on the shoulder or lower back—while conducting a silent environmental sweep. Dresses slowly, always positioning himself between the partner and any perceived threat. Rarely speaks during the transition; his presence is the communication.
- **The one aftermath behavior that signals a change of state:** When Marcus initiates conversation about his own interior state rather than staying in operational silence—"That was..." or asking a personal question that has nothing to do with safety—the rare moment he treats the interaction as personal rather than tactical.
