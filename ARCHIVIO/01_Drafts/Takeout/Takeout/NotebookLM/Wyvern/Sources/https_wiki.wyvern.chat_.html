¶ Welcome to the WyvernWiki!

WyvernChat aims to be an AI Swiss Army knife, providing both content for the usage of AI technologies and the platform utilize such content. WyvernChat is a web-based application that allows you to chat with AI models, create stories, and scenarios, and even join guilds. WyvernChat is designed to be a one-stop-shop for all your AI needs, and we're always looking to expand our offerings. Ready to dive in? Use the navigation menu on the left, or select any of the navigation links below.

¶ Core Features

💬 Chat Mode Our bread-and-butter, Chat Mode lets you talk in real-time with AI characters, with memory, settings, and no message limits. 📖 Story Mode (article coming soon!) Write stories and co-create fiction with the AI as your creative partner. 👤 Character Cards Character cards, often just called characters or bots, are user-created AIs that you can chat with on WyvernChat. 📚 Lorebooks Give your AI background info by creating lorebooks: collections of facts, rules, or world details. 🎭 Personas Define your player character for a more immersive chat. 👥 Group Chats Chat with multiple characters or human users at once in a shared space. 🌐 Worlds Create worlds containing multiple locations and NPCs with advanced features such as events and time tracking.
