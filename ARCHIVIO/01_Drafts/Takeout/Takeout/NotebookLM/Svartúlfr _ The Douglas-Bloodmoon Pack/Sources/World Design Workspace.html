  • Approved workflow inclusion
  • Approved architecture report inclusion
  • Approved validation report inclusion
  • Runtime validation followed by review acceptance Insufficient evidence:
  • Brainstorming
  • Single mentions
  • Open questions
  • Speculation
  • Candidate concepts
  • Draft proposals
  • Preliminary audits If authority cannot be demonstrated: DEFER or REVIEW REQUIRED

Rejected Canon
