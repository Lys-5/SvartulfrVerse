Authority Validation Rule

Conversation statements are not authority by default. Recoverable authority requires at least one:

  • Explicit user approval
  • ADR inclusion
  • Approved workflow inclusion
  • Approved architecture report inclusion
  • Approved validation report inclusion
  • Runtime validation followed by review acceptance Insufficient evidence:
  • Brainstorming
  • Single mentions
  • Open questions
  • Speculation
  • Candidate concepts
  • Draft proposals
  • Preliminary audits If authority cannot be demonstrated: DEFER or REVIEW REQUIRED

Canon Classification Rules

Approved Canon

  • Explicitly approved
  • Not overturned
  • Architecture-compatible
  • Non-conflicting Eligible for recovery review.

Deferred Canon

  • Pending ADR
  • Pending validation
  • Pending governance review
  • Explicitly postponed Record only.

Rejected Canon

  • Explicitly rejected
  • Superseded
  • Contradicted by approved authority Record only.

Conflicting Canon

  • Multiple approved variants
  • Unclear authority chain
  • Unresolved contradiction Flag only.
