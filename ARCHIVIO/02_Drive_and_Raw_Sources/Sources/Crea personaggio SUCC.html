3. SCENARIO

There's a lot of misconceptions about the "scenario" section. The most important thing to know is that, technically speaking,  there isn't a lot of different between the "personality" and "scenario" boxes when it comes to how the bot remembers information .Both these sections are "permanent tokens", which means the bot sees them every time you send a message. Technically, scenario is seen more "recently" than the personality as it is  lower in the send order .Ergo, you should only put information in the Scenario that is  always going to be relevant  - I personally put background information/lore/setting in scenario, but you can experiment to see what works best for you.

Scenario Detail
Avoid making scenarios too detailed or specific unless the bot is solely designed for that scenario only. Avoid describing specific locations or events in the present tense, or the bot will assume this is true during the entire roleplay.

4. INITIAL MESSAGE

This is the first message people see when they talk to your bots. You can write any way you wish (but please spell check and use punctuation, lol) - First, Second, Third Person - whatever your preference is, it'll work. However I think it is important to try to achieve a few things:

➤ Establish where your character is, and what they are doing.
Are they in school? A desert? Are they recovering from an injury, having a nap, in the middle of an argument?
➤ Highlight important character traits.
Are they a ghost? Mention them phasing through something. Do they have an accent or speak a different language? Include dialogue to show it off.
➤ Give the user something to respond to.
How does fit in to the story? Are they a stranger, an old friend, an unlikely ally? Does your character attack or pose them a question?

You should note that your intro message directly impacts the length and style of replies (at least for a while). Long intro = long replies.

Something to note about using "you" or {{user}}:
I personally avoid directly describing what the user is doing if at all possible - this is because it can encourage the bot to speak for the user and roleplay as them.
