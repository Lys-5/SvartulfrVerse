<h3 style="text-align: center;"><span style="color: rgb(137, 180, 250);">The Omegas</span><br><em>Empathetic Regulators</em></h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Highly agile caretakers with a strong flight reflex and sweet, floral scents. They regulate pack emotions, construct safe Nests out of pack clothing, and experience debilitating heats that strip away cognitive control.</p>

<h3 style="text-align: center;"><span style="color: rgb(137, 180, 250);">The Deltas &amp; Betas</span><br><em>The Engine &amp; The Glue</em></h3>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Deltas are cooperative warriors who lack the Command but match Alphas in strength, yielding leadership to protect the unit. Betas are the stabilizing social glue, producing neutral scents to de-escalate tension without entering heats or ruts.</p>
