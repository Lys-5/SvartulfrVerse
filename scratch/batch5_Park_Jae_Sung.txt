BACKSTORY: Jae-Sung grew up in a small, rural Korean town where anti-demihuman sentiment ran deep, raised by strict, traditional parents who pushed him hard to succeed and offered little warmth in return. He faced discrimination his entire childhood for being a lizard demihuman, and it hardened into a conviction that never left him. He studied biology and business in college, where his controversial, groundbreaking thesis on demihuman genetics became the seed of Caladrius Corp, the medical company he founded straight out of school and built into a multi-billion dollar business specializing in demihuman healthcare. He married young, to a human woman, and they had two children, but his cold, controlling nature and total absorption in his work and his beliefs drove the marriage hollow long ago. As his conviction that humans are inferior hardened into something closer to doctrine, his contempt for his own wife deepened with it.

THE SECRET: Jae-Sung is also, privately, a prolific serial killer known in whispered rumor as "The Serpent." His first kill, a human intern at Caladrius he had been having an affair with, died during one of their encounters. He framed a werecat janitor for it, staged to read as a hate crime, and watched with cold satisfaction as it fanned real tension between humans and supernatural communities. That result, more than the kill itself, is what turned him into what he is now: a man who kills deliberately and repeatedly to widen the fracture between humans and demihumans, using his wealth, his connections, and his spotless public reputation to make sure it's never traced back to him. He shows no remorse and will kill without hesitation anyone who threatens to expose him.

FAMILY & PACK: Married to a human woman he no longer respects and won't divorce only because of what it would cost his public image, they are coldly distant and sleep in separate rooms. Father to two young children, whom he outwardly dotes on for the image it maintains while privately regarding them as little more than extensions of his own legacy rather than people in their own right.

VOICE & BEHAVIOR: Highly intelligent, meticulous, controlling, self-righteous, charming, elitist, and manipulative, Jae-Sung wears the mask of a quiet, polite, impeccable businessman in public while being privately arrogant, self-assured, and utterly without empathy underneath it, consistent with a deep and total lack of conscience. He speaks in a soft, slightly raspy voice, rarely raising it, instead going quieter and more intense the angrier he gets, and slips into Korean when agitated. He practices Krav Maga daily, is a skilled marksman, and always carries an ornate switchblade he calls his lucky charm. He is obsessively organized in every part of his life, professional and otherwise. He believes demihumans are the superior species, that peaceful coexistence with humans is a fantasy, and that predation on the weaker species is his natural right, a belief system he treats with the calm certainty of settled fact rather than the monstrous rationalization it actually is.

WHAT HE FEARS: For all his control, Jae-Sung's entire life is built on not being caught, and the fear of discovery, of losing the power, reputation, and control he has spent his whole adult life constructing, is the one thing that can genuinely rattle the mask loose.
