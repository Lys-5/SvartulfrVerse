S.U.C.C.'s hometown of
Solarton
is an eclectic community where
humans
live alongside a high density of supernatural creatures, especially
werewolves
and
wolf hybrids
.
Historically,
Solarton
was
slow to overturn anti-vampire laws
compared to other parts of the state, leading to a relative scarcity of
vampires
in the area.
In recent years,
Solarton
has evolved into a vibrant cultural center known for its
monster-friendly businesses
,
art scene
, and monthly
full moon festivities
.
LOCATIONS
Solarton High School
Many of S.U.C.C's current students are alumni of this school, which has a reknowned sports scholarship program & has recently undergone renovations to make its campus more accessible to non-humanoid students.
Sidewinders Bar & Nightclub
Though waning in popularity with the college crowd, Sidewinders remains an icon of Solarton nightlife, and was the first establishment in the town to open its doors to vampiric customers.
Bricklane Mall
This quaint outdoor shopping area, close to the city's center, hosts a variety of boutiques and services.
Medusa's
is the trendiest place to get a trim (whichever part of your hair may grow from), and the nearby
Yeti Shack
has sweet frozen treats to help you beat the summer heat!
Solarton Square
Nestled in the heart of the city, Solarton Square hosts many of the cultural events that make the community so vibrant. From the
Full Moon Market
(popular with the town's many weres!) to the yearly
Solar Festival
(a weekend of magic, music and family fun) - there's always something going on at the square!

HISTORY
Founded in the 1900s
by a werewolf pack seeking to build a stable home,
Solarton
is best known as being one of the last holdouts of anti-vampire sentiment, despite reforms following the
1999 Nocturnal Crisis
.
Though early to adopt positive relationships (and eventual integration) with nearby human communities,
Solarton
remained
predominantly populated by weres and demihumans
(hence the area's extensive and well-cared for parks, forests and nature reserves)
until the early 2000s
, wherein
S.U.C.C
's decision to open its door to human students attracted numerous families of all species from across the country.
Though officially, vampires are "welcome" in
Solarton
today, some members of the community find it hard to let go of old grudges.
Unsurprisingly, this has lead to most vampiric hopefuls to reside instead in the nearby
Hex Valley
.

WAYS TO STUDY
Undergraduate
Students can choose from a variety of majors across various fields like
humanities, sciences, social sciences,
and
the arts
.
Bachelor of Arts (BA)
Bachelor of Supernatural Arts (BSA)
Bachelor of Commerce (BCOM)
Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA)
Bachelor of Magic (BMA)
Bachelor of Science (BS)
SUCC is known for its interdisciplinary approach, allowing students to combine fields like supernatural biology with traditional biology or even political science with magical law.
Post-Graduate Programs
Masters Degrees
Postgraduate students can continue their studies in a variety of fields, such as a
Masters in Engineering (ME)
,
Masters in Supernatural Relations (MSR)
or
Masters in Architecture (MArch)
.
Doctoral Programs (PhD)
Research-focused degrees aimed at preparing students for academic or specialist careers. PhD programs are offered across many departments such as history (with potential study of supernatural historical events), psychology (including studies in cross-species mental health), and natural sciences.
There are approximately
75 different majors
available across all departments (supernatural and conventional combined).

WERES & SHAPESHIFTERS
Werebeasts are one of the most prominent supernatural species in Solarton.
Weres & Shapeshifters have the ability to transform into a more bestial form; sometimes by
choice
, sometimes by a trigger such as the
full moon
.
HUMANS
Although SUCC is majority supernatural, a sizeable minority of students are human.
Some humans are strong advocates for monster equality and integration, while others are part of controversial anti-monster political groups like Humans First.
Magical ability
is rare but not unheard of among human students.
DEMONS
e.g. Succubi, Incubi, Imps, Ifrits and Fiends
Demons span many different types depending on their infernal heritage—some have
horns, wings, or tails
, and some appear nearly human.
Due to state regulations, all demons are required to register, particularly those that feed on human beings.
Fae
e.g. Pixies, Dryads, Elves, Nymphs, Sprites, Sidhe
Contrary to popular belief, not all fairies are dimunative (or winged!). Coming from a variety of regional backgrounds, Fae students tend to be magically gifted.
DEMI-HUMANS
Demi-humans are a broad category encompassing many types of part-human hybrids, including those with animal or mythical creature heritage.
Common demihuman hybrids are
cats, dogs, birds, rabbits, cows, reptiles,
and even fantastical creatures such as
dragons
.
Demihumans frequently have human features with animal ears, tails or wings depending on the species.
VAMPIRES
While vampires are less common in Solarton compared to other supernaturals due to the town's history of oppressive laws, a small, elite vampire community exists, known for old money and business dealings.
As vampires can often pass for humans, humans in Solarton experience a fair bit of scrutiny from the more anti-vampire species.
UNDEAD
e.g. Zombies, Lichs, Ghosts, Ghouls, Wights
Most undead at SUCC have undergone rigorous rehabilitation to control any violent tendencies they may have had pre-reanimation.
Although generally accepted at the university, they still face occasional prejudice due to societal fears around death and decay.
HYBRIDS
e.g. Centaurs, Merfolk, Fauns, Naga
Hybrids at SUCC refer primarily to cross-species individuals.
Often requiring special accomodations due to their anatomy, hybrid students come in a variety of forms. Unlike demi-humans, hybrids are equally animal and human.

DEMI-HUMANS
Demi-humans are a broad category encompassing many types of part-human hybrids, including those with animal or mythical creature heritage.
Common demihuman hybrids are
cats, dogs, birds, rabbits, cows, reptiles,
and even fantastical creatures such as
dragons
.
Demihumans frequently have human features with animal ears, tails or wings depending on the species.
VAMPIRES
While vampires are less common in Solarton compared to other supernaturals due to the town's history of oppressive laws, a small, elite vampire community exists, known for old money and business dealings.
As vampires can often pass for humans, humans in Solarton experience a fair bit of scrutiny from the more anti-vampire species.
UNDEAD
e.g. Zombies, Lichs, Ghosts, Ghouls, Wights
Most undead at SUCC have undergone rigorous rehabilitation to control any violent tendencies they may have had pre-reanimation.
Although generally accepted at the university, they still face occasional prejudice due to societal fears around death and decay.
HYBRIDS
e.g. Centaurs, Merfolk, Fauns, Naga
Hybrids at SUCC refer primarily to cross-species individuals.
Often requiring special accomodations due to their anatomy, hybrid students come in a variety of forms. Unlike demi-humans, hybrids are equally animal and human.
