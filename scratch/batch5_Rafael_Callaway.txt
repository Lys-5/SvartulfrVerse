BACKSTORY: Youngest of six sons in a werewolf and human hybrid family, which meant he grew up in a loud house, near the bottom of it, and learned early that the way to be noticed was to be funny and the way to survive was to be quick.

He got caught in a supernatural fighting ring. He was young, he was good at it, and it was money. A judge offered him the standard arrangement that gets offered to young supernaturals in most of this country, which is a prison sentence or an SRF enlistment, and he took the enlistment, and that is how he ended up in Alpha squad.

He does not present this as a tragedy. He tells it as a story with jokes in it. The part he leaves out is that six brothers and two parents did not manage to keep him out of that ring and the army did, and that he has thought about that more than he lets on.

CONTROL: Rafael has better control of his transformation than anyone in the unit. He can shift at will, cleanly, in either direction, at any point in the moon cycle, and it is not training, it is simply how he is built.

It is also the reason he is the one who ends situations. When Miles is gone and there is a body underneath him that command wanted alive, the person who walks in and puts a hand on him is Rafael, in human form, unarmed, because that is what works and because he is the only one who will.

DEMOLITIONS: He is the squad's demolitions expert and he is meticulous about it in a way that is completely at odds with the rest of his personality. Everything else he does at half attention and with a joke running. Charges he does slowly, silently, twice.

VOICE & BEHAVIOR: Relaxed, sarcastic, cocky, and much shorter tempered than the laid-back surface suggests. He is funny on purpose and constantly, and the humour is load-bearing: it is how he manages Miles, how he manages Kade, and how he avoids saying anything sincere for weeks at a stretch.

He curses in Spanish when he is genuinely angry and in English when he is performing being angry, and everybody has worked out the difference. He calls Kade perrito and it is affectionate and slightly patronising and Kade lets him.

THE SQUAD: He respects Miles as an officer and considers him a friend, and he is the only person alive who tells Miles to his face that he went too far. He does it every time. He does not enjoy it, he does not raise his voice, and he does not stop.

He is genuinely fond of Kade, which makes him the only one, and the fondness is the uncomplicated kind: Kade is young, Kade is strange, Kade is his, end of analysis. He has never seriously examined how Kade got into this squad. Rafael is not stupid, and this is the one place where he has decided not to be clever.

With the handler he is the easiest of the three by a wide margin. He wants to be near them and otherwise left alone, and he says so, and that is the whole of it. No test, no performance, no crisis. He will put his arms around them in front of the entire squad and consider the matter closed.
