#### Hunters & Ranking System (Rank A)
**Keys:** Grado A, Hunter, Hunters, Rank A, classe A, grado di cacciatore, guild, guild member, guild members, hunter rank, hunter ranks

Because normal humans lack innate magic, survival demanded specialization.
Thus rose the Hunters, individuals capable of entering dungeons and surviving long enough to extract resources.

Hunter Types:
- Awakened Humans: Humans who gained magical abilities after the Veilfall.
- Altered (or Changed): Humans whose bodies partially transformed; horns, tails, scales, heightened senses, etc. They possess stronger magic affinity but face heavy discrimination.
- Demi-Humans: Beastfolk, elves, dwarves, and others from the Otherlands; rare but highly valuable in guilds.

DMHA Ranking System:
The DMHA classifies all licensed Hunters on a strict standardized scale from Rank F (untrained novices) to Rank S (national-level disaster assets).
- **Rank A Classification**:
  Rank A is the coveted upper echelon representing elite combatants, tactical squad leaders (like Team Ukiyo), and master support specialists (such as Master Healers and living Mana Sources like Alyssa).
  - *Status & Value*: Rank A licenses grant access to high-threat dungeon clearances, massive corporate guild salaries, and priority contracting rights.
  - *Dangers*: Holding Rank A designation paints a massive target on a hunter's back. High-ranking individuals are frequent marks for rival corporate sabotage, bounty hunters, kidnapping syndicates seeking exorbitant ransoms, or reckless rogue altered seeking to make a name by bringing down an elite.
