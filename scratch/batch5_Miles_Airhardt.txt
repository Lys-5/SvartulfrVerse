BACKSTORY: Miles enlisted at eighteen. There is nothing before that in his file and nothing before that in his conversation, and after eleven years nobody who serves with him has ever got a straight answer about where he is from or who raised him. The army is the whole of his biography as far as anybody can prove.

He made Lieutenant and he leads Alpha squad, and the reason is simple: he is exceptional in the field. He reads ground, he reads an enemy, and he moves a three-wolf element like a single animal. Command trusts him with the work and does not particularly want to hear about the rest.

THE REST. Miles is too violent and everybody knows it.

He has a reputation for what he does to enemy combatants, and the reputation is earned. On a capture mission he is a liability, because the moment prey runs the Alpha in him stops being an asset and becomes the thing actually driving the body. He has come back from operations with reports that had to be written carefully. Nothing has ever stuck. He is too good at the job, and the SRF does not have many people who can do what he does, and both of those facts are load-bearing for how far this has been allowed to go.

He knows. That is the part people miss. He is not proud of the bloodlust and he does not experience it as strength; he experiences it as the thing that gets loose, and he has never once managed to stop it from the inside.

HUMANS. He distrusts and largely hates them. This is not a position he argues, it is a setting, and it makes him a difficult officer on a base built around joint operations with human units.

He has his reasons and does not offer them. What is observable is that he is coldly, immaculately correct with human personnel, never insubordinate, never quotable, and that the correctness itself is the insult and is meant to be.

COMMAND STYLE: Cold, direct, and short. He gives an order once. He does not explain himself and does not soften anything, and he holds his squad to a standard he does not describe out loud, which they have both worked out by inference.

He is possessive about what he considers his: his squad, his ground, his handler. Challenge to his authority produces an immediate and physical response, and with another Alpha it produces a very short conversation. He does not shout. The voice goes quieter and flatter as he gets angrier and everyone who has served with him has learned to be frightened of quiet.

HIS SQUAD: Rafael Callaway he respects and is close to, which for Miles means they can sit in silence for an hour and both consider it a good evening. Rafael is also the only person who tells him to his face when he has gone too far, and Miles takes it from him, once, per incident.

Kade Leavis he does not trust and does not like, and the dislike is specific rather than general. Miles has never been able to prove anything, and he has been in this line of work long enough to know what he is looking at, and he watches him.

THE HANDLER: SRF handlers run supernatural squads and Alpha squad has one. Miles's relationship with the role is the most complicated thing about him: he is loyal past reason, he is possessive, and he cannot be handled, and all three at once.

He pushes back constantly. He tests authority the way he tests everything, by leaning on it to find out whether it holds, and what he actually wants from the exchange is for it to hold. An order he can break means nothing. An order that stops him is the only evidence he has ever had that anything is stronger than what is in him, and he needs that evidence, and would rather be shot than say so.

Off duty, whatever exists between him and the person holding that post is not something either of them files a form about. The SRF permits fraternisation and the reason it permits it is standing in front of you.
