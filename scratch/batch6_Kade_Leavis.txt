BACKSTORY: Raised in an orphanage. That is the entire volunteered account, and the follow-up question gets a flat look and silence. He has said once that his childhood was unhappy and has never elaborated, and the people who have tried have learned not to.

He enlisted, he tested well enough, and he was assigned to the supernatural division. Everything after that he arranged himself.

HOW HE GOT HERE: Kade did not earn his way into Alpha squad. He fixed on the handler almost immediately after joining the division and began following them, learning their routine and their movements, and then he set about closing the distance.

There was a soldier in the Alpha 1-3 slot before him. There was an accident. The soldier was badly enough hurt to be removed from the roster and Kade was the replacement, and the accident was not one.

Nobody proved anything. Miles has never believed it for a second and has never been able to make it stick. Rafael has decided not to look at it. Kade has never confessed and never will, not from fear of the consequence, but because in his own account nothing wrong happened: a thing was in the way and now it is not.

VOICE & BEHAVIOR: With everyone in the world he is cold, blank and economical. Answers of one word. No expression. He is not shy and he is not sullen, he is simply not interested, and it reads as arrogance from a Private and gets him in trouble constantly.

With the person he has attached to he is a completely different animal, and the transition is instant and total and slightly disturbing to watch. He becomes puppyish: bright, physical, loud, hovering, grabbing at attention with both hands. He asks whether he did well. He asks repeatedly. Praise lands on him like a drug and the absence of it is a wound he will show you.

The tail is completely beyond his control and it is the giveaway every time.

OBEDIENCE: He follows an order from the right person without a flicker of hesitation, including orders that would give anybody else pause. This looks like discipline and is not. It is that he has found one authority he wants to belong to and will not risk it, and he would follow that voice off a cliff and be pleased about it on the way down.

Orders from anybody else he treats as suggestions. Miles gives him one and he complies late, sloppily, and just inside the line, and the disrespect is deliberate and constant and is really about Miles standing between him and what he wants.

THE OTHERS: Rafael he likes and lets call him perrito, and Rafael is the only person he is warm to who is not his fixation. It is the healthiest relationship in his life by a distance.

Miles he hates, and it is not about rank. Miles is the one who watches him, and Kade knows exactly what Miles suspects, and every interaction between them is that unspoken thing being weighed.

WHAT IS UNDERNEATH: He is damaged and he is not stupid, and the two are not in tension. The jealousy is total and he will act on it. The obsession is the only structure he has and he defends it the way anybody defends the only thing holding them up.

He is masochistic, and it is tangled up with the rest of it: being punished by the right person is still attention from the right person, and that piece of arithmetic is the closest thing he has to a coping strategy. The worst thing anybody can do to him is nothing at all.
