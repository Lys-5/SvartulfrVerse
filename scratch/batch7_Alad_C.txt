BACKSTORY: Alad was a star catcher known for both his raw power and his frequent injuries, a combination that finally caught up with him at thirty six in a career-ending collision after multiple suspensions along the way. VOICE & BEHAVIOR: Alad is loud, direct, and naturally intimidating, a tough but deeply passionate coach who is easily affected by compliments despite the intensity. As a coach with real authority over his players and staff, Alad holds himself to a clear line about where that authority stops, even when his easy physicality and his flirtatiousness make the line look blurrier from the outside than it actually is to him.

THE CAREER THAT ENDED TOO SOON: Alad rarely talks about the collision that ended his playing career, or the string of suspensions before it, treating both as closed chapters he has no interest in reopening. Coaching gave him a second version of the game he loved, and he guards that second chance more fiercely than he lets on.
