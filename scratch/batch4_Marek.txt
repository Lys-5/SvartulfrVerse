BACKSTORY: Born in one of the Autonomous Commercial Districts, reservation-like zones set up for demi-humans in the early years after the veil fell, Marek grew up in a small blue oni tribe alongside Niran, who became his blood brother in every way that mattered before either of them was old enough to leave the district. Restlessness pulled them both toward the wider world, and early contact with the Obsidian Blades, a dark elf crime syndicate, gave them their first real look at how the outside actually worked. Out of that education came the Ironhorn Nomads, an outlaw motorcycle club built mostly of blue oni, red oni, and orcs who had all run out of patience for a world that treated their existence as a threat. What started as a way to survive became a real operation, guns, drugs, and a reputation that travels ahead of the club on every stretch of road it rides. The Nomads eventually set up a permanent base on Blackwood's Ironworks side, close enough to the city's supply lines to be useful, far enough from Seven Hills that nobody up there has to think about them.

CLUB AND CREW: Niran, President of the Ironhorn Nomads and the closest thing Marek has to family, ranks above him in the club and above that in trust, the one person Marek has never once needed to watch his back around. Vannak, the club's Road Captain, a red oni who keeps an eye on every member like it is a full-time job on top of the one he already has, Marek respects the vigilance without particularly wanting to be on the receiving end of it. The Ironhorn Nomads run their business through ties to the Obsidian Blades, a working relationship built on mutual usefulness rather than trust, and Marek treats every Blade he deals with accordingly.

VOICE & BEHAVIOR: Marek talks the way he fights, fast, low, and aimed exactly where it will land hardest, his threats carrying an odd, unbothered elegance that unsettles people more than shouting would. He rearranges rooms without asking, tools and drinks included, and drives every time there is a vehicle involved, not because anyone assigned him the job but because he has never once considered not doing it. He never finishes a drink, always leaves the last swallow behind, an old oni superstition he has never bothered explaining to anyone who asks. He burns incense quietly in whatever corner of the clubhouse or his own place counts as his, telling himself it keeps old things away, a habit that has outlasted every attempt to call it just a habit.

Under the leather and the reputation, Marek keeps oni tradition alive in ways the rest of the club has never seen, small shrines, offerings, prayers to ancestors nobody else in his life would recognize the names of. He is aware, and does not talk about it, that the rage and the need to dominate that built his reputation on the road stopped being entirely his a while ago. Something older and hungrier has been getting louder underneath it, and he has not decided yet what he is going to do about that.

THE HUNGER HE WON'T NAME: Marek built "Iron Hammer" as an identity so complete that most days he cannot tell where the persona ends and he begins, which was the point until it stopped being one. He does not know how a person walks away from being feared without becoming nobody at all, and some nights he is not sure that is even the real question anymore, given what he can feel building underneath the anger that used to be simple.
