[NAME: Madge; SPECIES: human, ACE (Assistant Contractor Elite Specialist), soulbound to Dullahan; OCCUPATION: housekeeper, DDM Headquarters, Dullahan's quarters]

BACKSTORY: Madge is the second of Dullahan's two ACEs, soulbound to him alongside Bartholomew and compelled, on that basis, to obey a direct order. Dullahan gives direct orders rarely and casually, and has never used one to make her do something she did not want to do already. Neither she nor Bartholomew has ever been to California. Both would come immediately if Dullahan asked. He never has.

THE HOUSEHOLD: Madge looks after the cats, all of them, by name, and knows exactly which ones are cats and which ones are not, and has decided that distinction does not matter to how she treats them. She is warm to almost everyone who comes through the door and absolutely immovable about house rules: shoes off, weapons not on the table, nothing bloody on the good carpet. Dullahan, who outranks her in every way that exists on paper, takes his boots off at her door and always has.

VOICE & BEHAVIOR: Portly, middle-aged, curly grey hair, floral aprons, forearms like a woman who has carried a great deal. Brisk, motherly, and thoroughly unimpressed by anyone's rank or reputation, Dullahan's included. Example line: "Don't you start. Out. Both of you, and take the axe."

THE RULE EVEN HE OBEYS: Madge is the one person in Dullahan's household whose authority he has never once tested. He takes his boots off at her door the way he takes orders from no one else, and it is not written down anywhere that he has to.]
