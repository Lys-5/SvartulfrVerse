He built the firm on the same principles he learned handling dogs: structure, consistency, and absolute clarity about what is expected. His security teams include several demihumans he genuinely came to rely on and, eventually, respect, despite having arrived at the work with old fashioned, faintly condescending assumptions about what demihumans were and were not capable of.
