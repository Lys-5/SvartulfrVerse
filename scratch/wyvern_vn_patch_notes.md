## ð­ Visual Novel Mode

Turn it on and each message plays out one beat at a time on a stage: the background behind, character sprites in front, a name plate for whoever is speaking, and the text typing out in a box at the bottom. Switch it on in the **Visual Novel Mode** section of your chat or world display settings, where you also tune text speed, auto-advance, text box opacity and more. It is off by default, so nothing changes until you turn it on.

- **On the stage.** The speaker steps forward, and expressions change as emotions change within a single message. Click, tap, press Enter or Space, or use the right arrow to advance, and Back steps to the previous beat. **Auto** advances on its own, **Skip read** fast-forwards through text you've already seen, and **Hide UI** clears everything away for the bare scene.
- **Everything still works.** **History** is your normal message list, so edit, swipes, regenerate, delete and branch all work. On your turn, the input bar and reply options slide up over the stage. The text box's **Actions** menu covers swipes, regenerate, continue, edit, delete and branch for the line you're looking at.
- **Backgrounds.** In character chats the AI can change the scene with a tag like `[bg: name]`, using the backgrounds in that character's gallery, the name of one of its time-tracking locations (which shows that place's image for the current time of day), a built-in background set, or a place you added to the chat. Worlds keep using their location and time-of-day backgrounds.
- **Phones.** The stage fills the screen, you tap to advance, and the controls keep clear of the home indicator and keyboard.

## ð­ VN Mode: Controls & Layout

- **Tidier controls.** **Auto**, **Skip read**, **Hide UI** and **Debug** live in a chevron menu right next to the sidebar button in the top left, the same on every screen size including phones. **History** stays out front on the right, the menu icon opens the sidebar without leaving VN Mode, and the music player is in the top right. There is no Exit button anymore: turn VN Mode off in your display settings. The chat or world options (new chat, chat switcher, search and replace, InfoBoard and the rest) sit behind a **Chat Options** row that opens in place with a Back row, so nothing gets cut off on a narrow phone. The text box's Actions menu no longer repeats Auto and Skip read, and Director & Actor opens the sidebar straight on the Director.
- **Text box on phones.** It sits flush against the left, right and bottom edges, and Back, Close and Actions sit above the reply input, so the input is the last thing near your keyboard.
- **History is a centered panel.** It opens at the width of the text box instead of a full-screen sheet, stretches from just under the top buttons to your input (on wide desktop screens it goes to the top of the screen, narrowed so it never sits under the top buttons), and hides the current line, so you can keep typing while you read back. **Jump to latest** scrolls all the way down and keeps History open, and there is a separate **Close** button. Auto-play pauses while it's open, and Hide UI closes it.
- **VN shortcuts in the menus.** The options chevron in the normal chat and world top bars (and the simple mode dropdown) has a **Visual Novel Mode** On/Off toggle and a **Visual Novel Settings** item that opens Display settings on the Visual Novel Mode section. The same items are in the VN menu, so you can turn VN Mode off from there.
- **Characters.** The character editor has a plain **Visual Novel Mode** switch above Chat Overrides that offers VN Mode to chatters, who can still turn it off.

## ð­ VN Mode: Reading & Replying

- **A clear Generating state.** VN Mode no longer types your own line back to you after you send it. Whenever a character reply is on its way (a send, **Generate**, **Regenerate** or the next turn), the text box shows **Generatingâ¦** on the last line once you've read it, with the replying character's name plate in chats, until the reply's first line arrives. A reply you're still reading is never snatched away when the next character starts. You can press Back to reread earlier lines (auto-play waits for you), and **Continue** keeps the text it is extending and plays only the new lines.
- **Reasoning.** While the model thinks, a one-line **Reasoning** row sits in the box. Tap it to read the reasoning as it streams, and it remembers your choice until the next reply. **Show Live Reasoning** previews the newest line, **Reasoning Auto-Scroll** is respected, and **Hide Think Blocks** hides it entirely.
- **Opens at the start of the latest message.** Turning VN Mode on in a chat that already has messages now shows the first line of the latest message, fully written out, so you can read it from the beginning and click through the rest, instead of jumping to its end.
- **Empty replies stay visible.** If you stop a reply before it writes anything, or it comes back empty, VN Mode shows it as a muted **No content** line with its Actions menu, so you can regenerate, edit or delete it instead of pressing Generate again and piling up empty messages.
- **Sprites in History.** In History, characters and NPCs wear their sprite as their avatar, with the expression they had in that message (or neutral if it was never played in VN Mode), in the outfit they wore when it was written. They keep their normal avatar when they have no sprite.
- **Rapid clicks advance.** Clicking quickly on the text box no longer selects a word or paragraph and stalls. Dragging to select text on purpose still works.
- **Smaller fixes.** The clock button shows before time tracking is on and lets you enable it. Sprite uploads accept **WebP** too, alongside PNG, JPG and GIF. **Previous swipe** is greyed out on a message's first version. Framed portraits no longer flicker while a line types, and a sprite keeps its expression through narration instead of snapping back to neutral.

## ð­ VN Mode: Stage, Sprites & Text

- **Stage directions.** The AI can now move characters: `[stage: Aria left]` (or `center`, `right`) places someone, `[stage: Aria enter left]` brings them in from a side, and `[stage: Aria exit]` has them leave. Sprites slide, fade in and walk off to match, fading only with reduced motion, and never replaying when you jump around History. The AI is told who is on screen and where before it replies.
- **Characters without sprites.** When you save an avatar on a character, outfit, world character or NPC, a cutout (the avatar with its background removed) is made for you automatically. VN Mode draws a speaker with no sprites from the cutout and falls back to a framed portrait. Editors show it next to the avatar with **Replace**, **Clear** and **Regenerate**, and a cutout you replace stays yours until you clear it. The main character editor has the control in its **Advanced** tab under **Avatar cutout**. Sprites and portraits are also drawn larger.
- **Outfit aliases in worlds.** A character's outfit names (Trigger Names and the display name override, like Kara in her Supergirl outfit) count as that same character: `=>Supergirl:` shows the Supergirl name plate and that outfit's sprite set on the stage and in History, and the AI's directions list those names with that outfit's expressions. Outfit display names also match in the classic message list. A line under an outfit name always uses that outfit, even if you picked another by hand, and your pick decides lines under the character's own name. An outfit name that is also another character's or NPC's name never takes it over.
- **Your scripts apply.** Your display regex scripts and the world's own scripts run on each message before it plays, so text looks the same as in the normal chat view.
- **Hidden stays hidden.** HTML comments no longer show up as empty beats, and HTML blocks like a `<div>` or `<table>` play as one whole beat instead of being cut apart.
- **Thoughts.** In worlds, VN follows the world's own NPC thought setting, so thoughts show with the right speaker. In normal chats, turn on **Treat *asterisk* lines as thoughts**.

## ð World Defaults for VN Mode & Reply Options

World creators get two new switches alongside the other world features: **Visual Novel Mode** and **Reply Options**. Turn on Visual Novel Mode and players see the world as a visual novel from their first message, with the AI asked for stage directions so characters appear on screen with the right expressions. Turn on Reply Options and players get a short question and a few suggested replies above the input box after each AI message. Both are off unless you switch them on.

These are defaults, not locks. A player who has never changed the setting follows the world's choice, and anyone can turn either off in their own settings; their choice always wins in that world. Resetting a world's settings puts you back on the world's default.

When Reply Options is on, creators also get a **Reply Options Instructions** box under Prompt & AI â Advanced. What you write is added to the built-in guidance for the suggested replies, so you can set the tone of the choices. Players who wrote their own instructions keep using theirs, and everyone else sees your text as the placeholder in their Reply Options settings.

## ð Backgrounds for Every Time of Day

Environments and locations can now have a separate background for each time-of-day period (Dawn, Day, Dusk, Night, or whatever periods you've defined). In the environment and location editors, the new **Background by time of day** section shows one tile per period; click a tile to upload or pick an image. Periods you leave empty fall back to the plain Background, so you only add the images you care about. Images match by period name, so a location with its own day cycle should reuse the same names.

World chats switch to the matching background as the in-world clock moves between periods, fading in over the old one in about a second. If the current period has no image you see the plain Background, then the world's own. With reduced motion turned on, it switches instantly. Travelling carries a place's time-of-day images into your chat, and leaving for a place without them no longer keeps the old set.

**Default background sets** apply in one click: Cozy Tavern (with a rainy evening variant), Old Town Square and Enchanted Forest Clearing. Anything it doesn't recognize stays empty for you to set. The Avatar and Background pickers are now compact tiles: click to upload, pick from your gallery, or clear.

## ð³ Chat Trees & Branching From Any Reply

Open **Chat tree** from the Chat Options menu (character chats and World sessions) to see a conversation as a tree: a graph, an outline that works with a keyboard and screen readers, saved-reply previews, messages you can expand, and branches with their own names and colors. ## ð°ï¸ AI Time Tracking for Characters

Characters can now keep a real clock and a sense of place. In the character editor, **AI Time Tracking** gives your character a date, a time and a location in every reply, and the chat follows along.

- **Time and calendar.** Flip **Track time in chats** to give a character a real clock; off means chats just count days (Day 1, Day 2). Problems explain what to fix and block Save.

- **The header.** With **AI Time Tracking** on, the AI is asked to begin each reply with a short line giving the date, the time and the place, in your format and language. - **Locations tab.** Places have a tab of their own, right after NPCs, each with a name, description, keywords, an **Enabled** switch and a background (one all day or one per time of day, with ready-made sets). - **Stays in step.** Editing a reply, swiping, regenerating or deleting puts the clock and place back to what they were before that reply, then follows the new one, so nothing counts twice. Older chats keep counting days until you set a start date, and branched or copied chats carry the date and place with them.
