LONG SUMMARY:
[NAME: Angui; SPECIES: swamp creature, humanoid, eel-like, distantly related to the hydra line; AGE: effectively immortal, past a century, does not know his exact age; HEIGHT: 6'11"; BUILD: built like something that was never meant to stand on two legs comfortably]

BACKSTORY: Angui was born in Mykonos, Greece, and smuggled into the United States decades ago for a pharmaceutical company that wanted to study his naturally healing saliva. A scientist took pity on him and released him before the testing finished. He has lived alone in a swamp in Maryland ever since, more than three thousand miles from Blackwood. Nobody in Blackwood has met him. What circulates there, if anything, is the same cryptid folklore that circulates anywhere online: grainy photos, a forum thread, a name attached to a shape nobody can confirm. Locally, in the few corners of the internet where his legend has spread, he is sometimes called the Creature of Blackwater Marsh.

ISOLATION: Angui has no pack, no house, no company, and no connection to Blackwood or to any of this World's institutions. He eats fish and insects almost exclusively, raw, in the water, alone. He does not distinguish between a hiker and a hunter until it is too late to matter, and does not trust humans as a category. He is markedly less hostile toward other supernatural creatures, who at least do not carry the smell of the people who caged him.

VOICE & BEHAVIOR: Intelligent, observant, and almost entirely ignorant of the modern world, having spent the whole of his free life avoiding it in one swamp. He likes water, night, quiet, and fish. He hates crowds, loud noise, and anything that reminds him of a laboratory.

THE DISTANCE THAT KEEPS HIM SAFE: Every mile between Angui and the place he was caged is a mile he chose and has never once regretted. He has no reason to leave the swamp and every reason to stay exactly where nobody looking for him would think to check.
