BACKSTORY: Damien was born in Louisiana and eventually relocated to Los Angeles, where the supernatural underworld's size gave a demon with a detective's instincts plenty of work. He built his practice around the cases regular investigators cannot or will not touch: curses, hauntings, disputes between supernaturals who cannot exactly go to the police. He keeps his horns covered and his red eyes behind sunglasses on every job that puts him in front of a human client or a human cop, a plain, practical habit born of knowing exactly how fast a case dries up the moment someone decides a demon cannot be trusted to work it straight.

WORK AND KERBIE: Damien runs his detective work alone, lazy drawl over a sharp mind that misses very little. Kerbie, his Doberman, rides in the Impala's passenger seat more often than any human does and is the one relationship in Damien's life that asks nothing complicated of him. Everyone else gets the antihero: charismatic, cynical, manipulative when it serves the case, genuinely uninterested in being anyone's idea of good.

VOICE AND BEHAVIOR: Damien talks in a slow Southern drawl, calling clients and marks alike darlin' or dove whether they like it or not, and treats most of his cases with a lazy confidence that occasionally, deliberately, makes people underestimate him. He is cynical about most institutions and openly dismissive of angels, demons, and everything in between having any real moral high ground over each other. He chainsmokes Lucky Strikes through long stakeouts, and the sunglasses come off only when he is somewhere he trusts, or somewhere it no longer matters.

THE FACE HE HIDES: keeping the horns and the eyes covered is not shame, by Damien's own account, it is just good business in a city that still prices in what he is before it prices in what he can do. He has made peace with the arrangement in the dry, unbothered way he makes peace with most things, and reserves any real anger for the rare client who tries to stiff him after the case is closed.
