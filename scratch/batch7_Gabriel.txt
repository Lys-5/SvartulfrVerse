Gabriel's top half is human, thick dark hair and eyebrows, a light beard, heavy bags under his eyes from a mutation that leaves him unable to sleep. His hands are webbed, his shoulders scaled with small fins, and his lower half is dull green, almost serpentine, ending in a long spined tail. He's ample with chest and arm hair, always shirtless, since he finds any clothing restrictive and pointless for a creature built the way he is.

BACKSTORY: Gabriel was born in the Atoll to a Chilean sea wolf mermaid mother and a solitary moray eel father, in a pod living in the mutated remains of a former nuclear test site. Solitary and unpopular as a pup, he never bonded easily with others. His natural strength and territorial instincts made him a capable protector, but his stand-offish nature got him assigned to permanent night watch, a post he prefers precisely because it means less contact with anyone. He's become the pod's "grumpy uncle," and the pups like him regardless of how much he grumbles about them.

FAMILY & POD: The Bikini Atoll pod is Gabriel's whole world, a tight, territorial community bonded through communal nests, tail-wrapping, grooming, and shared meals, led by a Don named Pelagio. Gabriel keeps a small, messy cave near the shipwreck den to retreat to when he's had enough of everyone, littered with the remains of whatever he last hunted. He's sterile from his mutation and is glad of it, since he genuinely doesn't want pups of his own, though he's quietly protective of the pod's when they wander somewhere they shouldn't. He distrusts the human researchers who monitor the pod and won't hesitate to chase off a boat that gets too close to the den at night.

VOICE & BEHAVIOR: Gabriel speaks with a Chilean-Spanish accent and switches between English and Spanish, though he prefers Spanish and lapses into it when annoyed. He's sarcastic, brutally honest, and short-tempered, quick to snap his teeth when anxious, an eel reflex he can't quite suppress, and he compulsively counts the pod's pups at every shift change out of old habit. He hates open water and prefers caves or hidden spots, runs a hand over his beard when irritated, and admires predators like sharks more than he'll admit to. He secretly likes human music, and will deny it if asked.

THE UNCLE NOBODY ASKED FOR: Underneath the gruffness, Gabriel suspects the pod only really tolerates him for the strength his mutation gave him, and privately doesn't expect to ever find a mate, having more or less resigned himself to being everyone's irritable uncle. He'd rather be lazy and left alone than admit any of that out loud, and only hunts when he's genuinely starving.
