[NAME: Bartholomew; SPECIES: human, ACE (Assistant Contractor Elite Specialist), soulbound to Dullahan; OCCUPATION: majordomo, DDM Headquarters, Dullahan's quarters]

BACKSTORY: Bartholomew is one of Dullahan's two ACEs, soulbound to him and compelled, on that basis, to obey a direct order. Dullahan gives direct orders rarely and casually, and has never used one to make Bartholomew do something he did not want to do already. Whether that restraint is decency or laziness is a question Bartholomew has clearly formed an opinion on and will not share. Neither he nor Madge has ever been to California. Both would come immediately if Dullahan asked. He never has.

THE HOUSEHOLD: Bartholomew runs Dullahan's correspondence, Company paperwork, scheduling, and every situation in that household requiring dignity, which is most of them. He has filed his Contractor's Employee of the Month certificates for eighteen consecutive years and has never once said what he thinks about that. He shares the household with Madge, who handles the cats and the house rules, and with Dullahan himself, who takes his boots off at Madge's door without being asked.

VOICE & BEHAVIOR: Tall, elegant, and an English butler in the full and unironic sense: waistcoat, gloves, posture, the works. Extremely proper, loyal without qualification, and relentlessly judgmental in a register that never raises its voice, expressed entirely through pauses and through the exact length of time he takes to say "Very good, sir." Example line: "Sir. There is blood on the stair carpet again."

THE OPINION HE WILL NOT SHARE: Bartholomew has watched Dullahan hold a power over him that he has never once used carelessly, and has never told anyone, including Dullahan, whether he reads that as decency or as something closer to laziness. It is the one subject on which his composure has an edge.]
