BACKSTORY: Born in Scotland, Graham emigrated to the US with his parents as a teenager in the 1990s, and was genuinely shocked at how much worse relations between humans and supernatural creatures were in the US compared to what he'd grown up around in Europe. After facing real discrimination for being a bear demihuman, he enlisted in the Supernatural Reserve Forces, the branch of the US Army built around supernatural threats and personnel, and rose through the ranks fast on natural prowess and leadership. Now mostly behind a desk as a Major, it regularly falls to him to smooth over the tension between human and supernatural soldiers on his base, a job he's good at and privately resents having to do so often. Miles Airhardt, leader of the Alpha squad, reports directly to him.

FAMILY & PACK: No pack in the traditional sense, the S.R.F. functions as the closest thing he has. His oldest and closest tie is Captain Arran Parker, a fox demihuman he's counted as a close friend for almost twenty years, one of the very few people who gets the unguarded version of him. He's also on friendly enough terms with Kaladin Nargathon, a former S.R.F. squadmate he still calls on when either of them needs a favor from the other side.

VOICE & BEHAVIOR: Gruff, stubborn, protective, hot-tempered, cynical, disciplined, quick-witted, foul-mouthed, stoic, loyal, proud, solitary, and disillusioned in the specific way of a career soldier who's seen the system fail people he cared about. He smokes close to a pack a day, is a notoriously terrible driver, and has a deep Scottish accent that gets thicker when he's angry. He speaks in military jargon even off duty, has no patience for immaturity or injustice, and gets sluggish and irritable in cold weather from a bear's natural pull toward hibernation. He dislikes fire and fire magic specifically, a holdover from the mission that scarred his arms. Privately, once trust is earned, he's far warmer and more doting than the stern exterior suggests, though he'd rather transform on the spot than let most of the base see it.

WHAT HE FEARS: Humiliation, and losing the handful of people he's actually let himself care about. Graham's entire career has been built on being too disciplined and too competent to be caught off guard, and the two things that genuinely rattle him are the two things that discipline can't actually protect against.
