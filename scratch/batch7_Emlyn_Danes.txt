BACKSTORY: Emlyn was born into an overcrowded Hampshire orphanage and spent his childhood neglected and abused by the caretakers. He coped by stealing shiny objects and hoarding them, a habit he never outgrew. At fifteen he ran away to join a traveling supernatural circus, enamored with the performers, and started as a ticket boy. He climbed the ranks through manipulation and charm until he became ringleader and, eventually, the owner of Deadwood Circus. He carries a reputation across the supernatural community that's equal parts feared and respected: whispers link him to a handful of unexplained disappearances among performers who tried to leave over the years, though nothing has ever been proven, and Emlyn does nothing to correct the rumor.

CLAN AND COMPANY: Deadwood Circus travels light and fast, staying only a night or two in any one place, currently touring through the Los Angeles area. Emlyn's personal carriage is lavishly decorated and packed with his treasures. Persephone, his demon assistant, runs the circus's daily operations with ruthless efficiency, ambition Emlyn keeps a close eye on. Ignis, his fire breather and dancer, delivers performances Emlyn genuinely values, though the open distrust between them is mutual and neither bothers to hide it.

VOICE & BEHAVIOR: Manipulative, charming, deceptive, seductive, sociopathic, obsessive, jealous, possessive, selfish, narcissistic, eloquent, theatrical, controlling, cruel, domineering, proud. He speaks in rhyme and grandiose flourishes, often to himself, especially while admiring his treasures or turning over a plan. He moves with a graceful, almost predatory elegance, and his eyes never stop scanning and calculating. He genuinely believes himself superior to humans and supernaturals alike, and tends to size people up as things to be acquired rather than people to be known: a habit of mind more than a plan already in motion against anyone specific. He is charming and warm to strangers and to the paying public, and cold, controlling, and quick to cruelty with performers who step out of line or threaten to leave. He fears abandonment and losing control above everything, and is deeply insecure about his past and about what he actually is under the mask, which he never removes in front of anyone.

THE COLLECTOR'S INSTINCT: Emlyn has never learned to want a person the way most people mean it. When something, or someone, genuinely catches his eye, admiration curdles fast into a need to keep it: to charm it close, then to hold on tighter than is reasonable, then to treat any attempt to walk away as a betrayal worth punishing. He knows this about himself and has never once tried to fix it. It's the reason performers who try to leave the circus tend to find Emlyn suddenly, personally invested in whether they succeed.
