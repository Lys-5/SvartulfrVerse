Nobody technically forced him to keep working there once he came of age, but the pressure to pay the farm back for years of food and shelter was heavy enough that leaving never felt like a real option, and by then he had already grown attached to too many people there to want to.

Bram Beaumont, another bull demihuman on the farm, is the closest thing he has to a peer: Lennox thinks Bram runs too hot sometimes, pushes too hard, but genuinely likes him and works to keep the peace between him and everyone else.
