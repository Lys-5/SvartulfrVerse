## 1. Executive Summary & Sacred Lore Foundations

In the Svartúlfr Verse (Douglas-Bloodmoon Modern Fantasy universe), body art, ritual scars, and sacred marks are not merely decorative. They embody spiritual identity, ancient bloodline oaths, and celestial destiny. This specification establishes the binding canon for all runic tattoos, divine marks, and pack body art across characters, scenario prompts, visual assets, and lorebook records.

### 1.1 The 5th Day Lycanthrope Birth-Rune Ritual

The Birth-Rune ritual is one of the most sacred and unalterable ceremonial traditions of lycanthrope society. Celebrated exactly on the fifth day after the birth of a newborn cub, the ceremony is performed by the Clan Patriarch or pack elder to inscribe the newborn's Futhark rune, declaring their spiritual essence, temperament, and bond with fate.

The ritual requires three archaic and immutable components:

1. **The Sacred Needle (L'Ago Sacro)**:
   The inscription is performed exclusively using an authentic serpent tooth. The tooth symbolizes venomous wisdom, shedding of skin (transformation), and surgical precision.
2. **Cold Iron Ink (L'Inchiostro del Vincolo)**:
   The ceremonial ink is hand-mixed using purified sacred charcoal blended with micro-fine cold iron dust. Cold iron is critical: by anchoring the ink directly into the regenerating flesh of a lycanthrope, it permanently stabilizes the magical resonance of the rune and acts as an indestructible ward against demonic interference, astral corruption, and mind-control curses.
3. **The Anatomical Placement (La Sede)**:
   The birth rune is always inscribed on the inner left wrist (volar wrist, flexor side). This placement honors the side of the heart and the combat guard arm, leaving a distinct, slightly raised dark ceremonial keloid scar with a characteristic gunmetal sheen.

## 2. Divine Celestial Hip Marks of the Douglas-Bloodmoon Twins

### 2.1 The Prophecy of the Twin Wolves

Born simultaneously on April 22, 2005, during the tragic demise of their mother Nixara Bloodmoon (3rd White Moon), the twins Jasper and Alyssa embody the fulfillment of the ancient Bloodmoon prophecy:

```text
Tha er tungl ok sol risa saman i dreyra,
mun old hinna Fyrstu lida undir lok,
ok Vigit mun verda Konungr
eptir eitt hundrad ok fimm tigi vetra.

(Translation: When the moon and the sun rise together in blood,
the era of the Firstborn shall come to an end,
and the Bastion shall become King after one hundred and fifty winters.)
```

### 2.2 Strict Anatomical Invariants for the Twins

The sacred celestial marks of the twins are subject to non-negotiable anatomical rules:

1. **Alyssa Hvit Douglas Bloodmoon (Hvit Maniulfr)**:
   - **Sacred Mark**: The Divine Runic Mark of Mani (The White Moon).
   - **Anatomical Location**: **Strictly on the LEFT HIP** (iliac crest sweeping along the pelvic line).
   - **Visual Manifestation**: An elegant, outward-facing silver crescent moon intertwined with delicate Elder Futhark lunar glyphs.
   - **Luminescence**: When awakened or resonating with pack magic, it radiates a soft, pearlescent moonlight-silver sub-dermal bioluminescence.
   - **Sole Shared Tattoo**: The Gebo rune (`ᚷ`) on the inner left wrist, inscribed in cold iron ink.
   - **Exclusive Cosmic Title**: **Maniulfr** (Maniulf / White Moon Wolf).

2. **Jasper Svartr Douglas Bloodmoon (Svartr Solulfr)**:
   - **Sacred Mark**: The Divine Runic Mark of Sol (The Radiant Black Sun of Skoll).
   - **Anatomical Location**: **Strictly on the RIGHT HIP** (along the pelvic crest and lower oblique).
   - **Visual Manifestation**: An intricate radiant black sun medallion with angular solar flares and ancient Sowilo sun runes in dark obsidian black with burnt amber edges.
   - **Luminescence**: When activated, it ignites with deep molten-gold and dark solar embers, accompanied by subtle glowing cyan technomantic circuit traces.
   - **Sole Shared Tattoo**: The Gebo rune (`ᚷ`) on the inner left wrist, inscribed in cold iron ink.
   - **Exclusive Cosmic Title**: **Solulfr** (Sun Wolf).

### 2.3 Categorical Prohibitions & Purged Legacy Elements

To preserve canon consistency and eliminate legacy drift:

- **Absolute Prohibition of Dispersed Celestial Marks**: It is strictly forbidden to place celestial marks or divine symbols on the chest, collarbone, neck, hands, shoulders, or back wings.
- **No Wing Scars / Angel Wings**: The twins are pureblood terrestrial lupine shapeshifters, not winged or celestial beings.
- **Title Exclusivity**: The title Solulfr belongs exclusively to Jasper. Alyssa must never be called Solulfr; her title is strictly Maniulfr.
- **Sole Shared Tattoo**: The Gebo rune on the left wrist is the only shared tattoo between the twins.

## 4. Visual Reference Design & Aesthetic Shaders

### 4.1 Master Aesthetic Directives
- **Art Style Standard**: Masterpiece, best quality, semi-realistic niji style, painterly realism, anime-inspired realism, dark modern fantasy.
- **Single-Ear Anatomy Rule**: All demi-humans and lycanthropes possess exactly ONE pair of ears: their animal wolf ears atop the head. A secondary pair of human ears is strictly prohibited.
- **Cold Iron Ink Shader**:
  - Deep, matte charcoal-black pigment embedded with microscopic gunmetal-grey metallic flakes.
  - Distinct, slightly raised keloid border along the staves representing the healed serpent-tooth incision.
  - Metallic specular highlights catching grazing light with a gunmetal sheen.
- **Sub-Dermal Bioluminescence Shader**:
  - **Mani (Alyssa)**: Pearlescent silvery-white radiance (`#E2E8F0` to `#FFFFFF`) with an iridescent lavender-blue perimeter, diffusing smoothly from beneath fair skin.
  - **Sol (Jasper)**: Dark solar flame glowing molten amber-gold (`#F59E0B` to `#D97706`) with cyan technomantic energy conduits tracing outward into muscle tissue.
