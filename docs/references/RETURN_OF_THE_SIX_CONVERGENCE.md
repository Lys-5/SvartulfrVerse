## 5. Canonical Rules for Kaladin Nargathon (`Kári Hafgufa`) & Marcus Thornfield (`Brandr Eldstígi`) — Project BlackWolf Chained Firstborn

1. **Soul Pairing (6/6 Convergence Complete):**
   - **Kaladin "Blackwolf" Nargathon (`Subject Zero`, DCC Security Commander):** Houses the chained primordial soul of **Kári Hafgufa** (*Abyssal Maelstrom*, domain of freezing ocean tempests and abyssal pressure, mirrored in Kaladin's pale ice-grey eyes, glacial composure, and crushing containment over an internal maelstrom).
   - **Marcus "Iron" Thornfield (Vanguard Field Lead):** Houses the chained primordial soul of **Brandr Eldstígi** (*Fire-Strider*, domain of volcanic fury and primordial magma, mirrored in his call-sign `"Iron"`, coiled kinetic explosiveness, and hyperthermic `Berserker Internal Furnace` masked behind dry humor).
2. **Dr. Airen Nargathon's Secret Bio-Thaumaturgy vs Military Cover:**
   - **The Military Cover:** Even to U.S. Army and Supernatural Reserve Forces (S.R.F.) generals, Project BlackWolf was classified strictly as a genetic-enhancement program designed to engineer controllable military Enigmas on Squad Gamma-7.
   - **The True Mechanism:** **Dr. Airen Nargathon** (Kaladin's father and director of Project BlackWolf) was the sole conscious architect of the true ritual. Combining 9th-century False Ragnarök relics/fossilized tissues with compatible ancestral genomes (having married Kaladin's mother specifically for her latent Founding Bloodline genome, matched in Gamma-7 only by Marcus Thornfield's ancient soldier lineage), Airen performed a bio-thaumaturgic invocation that **dragged the dormant souls of Kári Hafgufa and Brandr Eldstígi into living operators** and locked them behind **sub-dermal cold-iron and cyber-runic neural cages** (the "cybernetic/bio-engineered implants" Jasper later spotted in Erik's encrypted Project BlackWolf archives).
   - **Why 6 of 8 Gamma-7 Operators Died:** The other six operators of Gamma-7 lacked Founding Bloodline resonance; the violent friction between the primordial Firstborn souls and the cold-iron neural cages burned out their bodies and drove them fatally feral. Only **Kaladin (18)** and **Marcus (19)** survived, left with mutated black shifted fur, crimson-flaring eyes under stress, `Founding Bloodline` + `Berserker Internal Furnace` physiology, and a "second animal sitting under the skin, permanently roused."
3. **The White Moon Resonance (Dr. Nixara Bloodmoon in 2004-2005 & Alyssa in 2019-2024):**
   - When the Army assigned **Dr. Nixara Bloodmoon** (3rd White Moon / 3rd incarnation of **Hvít**) as psychiatrist and psychologist to Gamma-7 in 2004-2005, both Kaladin and Marcus experienced something no medical report could explain: across the desk from Nixara and her scent of wild honey, **the raging "second animal" inside both of them stopped snarling against its cage and went completely calm**. Inside their cold-iron prisons, **Kári Hafgufa and Brandr Eldstígi had recognized the spirit and scent of their ancient beloved companion Hvít**. That shared peace is why all eight operators of Gamma-7 stood at the back of Nixara's funeral in dress uniform in April 2005.
   - Fourteen years later (2019), when **Alyssa Hvit Douglas Bloodmoon** (4th White Moon / 4th **Hvít**) presented as an Omega carrying her mother's unmistakable wild-honey scent, the chained souls of **Kári** (in Kaladin) and **Brandr** (in Marcus) went silent in her presence for the first time since Nixara's funeral. Both Kaladin (who personally commands Alyssa's detail and allows only her to call him `"Kaly"`) and Marcus (who leads Vanguard Security at Villa Douglas) feel the twenty-year torment under their skin settle into peace whenever Alyssa is near.
4. **Strict Epistemic Isolation & Triple Discovery Hook -> Harmonic Symbiosis Arc:**
   - **Hermetic In-Character Obliviousness:** Kaladin, Marcus, Erik, Malachia, Wulfnic, Zefir, and Ut are all **100% unaware in-character** that the "second animal" caged inside Kaladin and Marcus is actually **Kári Hafgufa** and **Brandr Eldstígi**.
